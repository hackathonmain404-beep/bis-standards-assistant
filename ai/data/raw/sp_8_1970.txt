SP 8:1970
ISI HANDBOOK OF MATHEMATICAL, PHYSICAL, CHEMICAL AND ENGINEERING TABLES
(Reaffirmed 2022)

1. SCOPE AND PURPOSE
1.1 A reference handbook containing mathematical, physical, chemical and engineering tables, intended as a quick reference for students and technical users. The handbook is designed as lookup and reference material rather than a narrative textbook.
1.2 The handbook derives data from Indian Standards and industrial product references with symbols and nomenclature aligned with ISO references.
1.3 Multiple reprints and amendment material are included, including Amendment No. 1 and addenda material.

2. MATHEMATICAL TABLES
2.1 Section 1 provides comprehensive mathematical tables including tables for logarithms, antilogarithms, natural logarithms, and exponential/hyperbolic functions.
2.2 Tables are provided for trigonometric functions, natural functions, circular arcs, radians, powers/roots, reciprocals, and mathematical constants.

3. PHYSICAL AND CHEMICAL TABLES
3.1 Section 2 provides physical and chemical tables describing physical and chemical quantities, units, symbols, and nomenclature aligned with relevant ISO references.
3.2 Physical constants, chemical properties, and standard values derived from Indian Standards and industrial reference data.

4. ENGINEERING TABLES
4.1 Section 3 contains engineering tables including structural steel and related tabular data such as steel sections, dimensions, weights, and engineering properties.
4.2 Engineering tables also provide comparison and reference tables for wires and cables, as well as fabricated steel products.
