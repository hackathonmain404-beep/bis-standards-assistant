IS 269:2015
ORDINARY PORTLAND CEMENT — SPECIFICATION
(Sixth Revision)

1. SCOPE
1.1 This standard covers the manufacture and chemical and physical requirements of 33 grade, 43 grade and 53 grade ordinary Portland cement.
1.2 It applies to ordinary Portland cement used in general civil engineering construction works.

4. CHEMICAL REQUIREMENTS
4.1 The ratio of percentage of lime to percentages of silica, alumina and iron oxide shall be between 0.66 and 1.02.
4.2 Insoluble residue shall not exceed 5.0 percent by mass.
4.3 Total sulphur content calculated as sulphuric anhydride (SO3) shall not exceed 3.5 percent by mass.

5. PHYSICAL REQUIREMENTS
5.1 Fineness: The specific surface of ordinary Portland cement tested in accordance with IS 4031 shall not be less than 225 m2/kg.
5.2 Setting Time: The initial setting time shall not be less than 30 minutes, and the final setting time shall not be more than 600 minutes.
5.3 Compressive Strength: The average compressive strength at 28 days shall not be less than 33 MPa for 33 grade, 43 MPa for 43 grade, and 53 MPa for 53 grade cement.

8. MARKING
8.1 Each bag of ordinary Portland cement shall be clearly marked with the manufacturer name, grade of cement, batch number, and the official BIS Standard Mark (ISI Mark).
