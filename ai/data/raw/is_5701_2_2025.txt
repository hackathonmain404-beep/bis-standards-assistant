IS 5701 (Part 2):2025
CODE FOR BREEDING, CARE, MANAGEMENT AND HOUSING OF LABORATORY ANIMALS — PART 2 LABORATORY RABBITS
(Second Revision, February 2025)

1. SCOPE
1.1 This standard lays down guidelines for the breeding, care, management, and housing of laboratory rabbits.
1.2 This standard is Part 2 of a ten-part laboratory-animal series under the Animal Husbandry and Equipment Sectional Committee, FAD 32, Food and Agriculture Division Council (ICS 65.020.30). Other parts in the series cover mice/rats, guinea pigs, hamsters, snakes, frogs, chicks, pigeons, mosquitoes, and other laboratory animals.

2. HOUSING AND ENVIRONMENTAL ENRICHMENT
2.1 Rabbits should be housed in stable, suitable groups when appropriate after weaning, with careful age and sex considerations.
2.2 Environmental enrichment includes provision of hay/roughage, chewable items, retreat/burrow-like spaces, visual barriers, and exercise opportunities.
2.3 The standard directly links environmental enrichment and exercise with normal skeletal and muscular development and overall animal welfare.

3. BEDDING AND FEEDING
3.1 Bedding provisions require dry, non-absorbent material placed in the nesting area, which must be stored in clean and dry conditions.
3.2 Feeding provisions mandate pelleted feed, grass hay/greens, gradual food changes, and monitored water consumption.
3.3 Feed-batch records, including manufacturing date and expiry information, are mandatory components of the feeding management provisions.

4. HEALTH, BREEDING AND WELFARE MANAGEMENT
4.1 Disease-control procedures, health monitoring, veterinary oversight, and facility-management requirements must be documented and maintained.
4.2 Breeding and reproductive biology provisions require humane care, with specific protocols for potentially painful or invasive procedures and humane endpoints in the animal welfare framework.
