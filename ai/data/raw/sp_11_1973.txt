SP 11:1973
RECOMMENDED SI UNITS FOR TEXTILES
(Reaffirmed 2017, First Reprint 1984)

1. SCOPE AND PURPOSE
1.1 This special publication lists recommended SI units for use in the textile industry and trade, with metric equivalents and specific application fields.
1.2 Formulated under the Physical Methods of Test Sectional Committee, TDC 1, this document provides guidance for changing over to a unified SI system in textile trade and industry to support consistent interpretation of measured textile characteristics.

2. UNITS AND CHARACTERISTICS TABLE
2.1 Table 1 maps textile characteristics to recommended SI units, metric equivalents, standard abbreviations, and fields of application.
2.2 Measured characteristics include fabric and yarn dimensions, yarn counts, mass per unit area, mass per unit length, twist, test length, and breaking load.
2.3 Examples of recommended SI units and abbreviations include millimetres (mm), centimetres (cm), grams per square metre (g/m²), grams per metre (g/m), turns per centimetre (turns/cm), turns per metre (turns/m), millinewtons (mN), and newtons (N).

3. TERMINOLOGY AND CONVERSION
3.1 The publication defines and explains measurement terms and unit relations relevant to textile testing and physical evaluation.
3.2 For conversion between traditional yarn counts and the tex system, users are directed to the specific relevant Indian Standard.
