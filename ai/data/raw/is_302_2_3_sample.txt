IS 302 (Part 2/Sec 3):2007
SAFETY OF HOUSEHOLD AND SIMILAR ELECTRICAL APPLIANCES
PART 2: PARTICULAR REQUIREMENTS — SECTION 3: ELECTRIC IRONS

1. SCOPE
1.1 This standard deals with the safety of electric dry irons and steam irons, including those with a separate water reservoir or boiler having a capacity not exceeding 5 litres, for household and similar purposes.
1.2 The rated voltage is not more than 250 V for single-phase appliances and 480 V for other appliances.
1.3 Appliances not intended for normal household use, but which nevertheless may be a source of danger to the public, such as appliances intended to be used by laymen in shops, in light industry and on farms, are within the scope of this standard.

3. DEFINITIONS
3.1 Steam Iron — An electric iron having means for producing and supplying steam to the textile while ironing.
3.2 Cordless Iron — An iron that is placed on a base during heating and removed from the base during ironing.

4. GENERAL REQUIREMENT
4.1 Appliances shall be constructed so that in normal use they function safely so as to cause no danger to persons or surroundings, even in the event of careless operation.

8. PROTECTION AGAINST ACCESS TO LIVE PARTS
8.1 Appliances shall be constructed and enclosed so that there is adequate protection against accidental contact with live parts.
8.2 Openings in live parts shall be designed so that testing finger probes conforming to IS 1401 cannot touch live electrical conductors.

11. HEATING
11.1 Appliances and their surroundings shall not attain excessive temperature in normal use.
11.2 For electric irons, the soleplate temperature shall be controlled by a reliable thermostat conforming to IS 302-1.
